 'This'

∊ is member of (which maps automatically). ⎕A is the uppercase alphabet. ⊂ cuts the data on its right where indicated by the mask on its left. The whole thing can also be written as ∊∘⎕A⍛⊂ 'ThisIsCamelCaseText'.

      (+⌿ ÷ ≢) [3 1 4
                1 5 9
                2 6 5
                3 5 8]
2.25 4.25 6.5

+⌿ sums the columns. ≢ is tally (number of rows). ÷ (divide) maps automatically over a list and a number. (+⌿ ÷ ≢) is an anonymous function; the column sum divided by the row count.

      ,∘≢⌸ 'GATTACA'
[
 'G' 1
 'A' 3

,∘≢ composes concatenation (,) with counting (≢), so for example 10 ,∘≢ 'abcd' is 10 4. ⌸ calls that composed function with each unique letter on its left and its indices on its right, pairing up letters with how many times they occur.
