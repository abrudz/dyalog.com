# Dyalog for Business

**Support for Business Applications**

Dyalog powers business critical applications all over the world. If you need help to
turn your idea into a product, training for your development team, or Service Level
Agreements to match the obligations that you have towards your customers, we can
help!

## Services

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   __Dyalog Support Service (DSS)__

    *Dedicated support for Dyalog users*

    The DSS offers maintenance and support services for Dyalog users of all
    [supported versions of Dyalog](https://dyalogprod.gos.dyalog.com/products/dyalog-versions/).
    All [commercial licences](https://dyalogprod.gos.dyalog.com/pricing-and-licences/commercial-licences/)
    include DSS; it can also be purchased separately. Issues reported by DSS users
    are prioritised, and regular software updates are provided.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Dyalog Support Service](https://dyalogprod.gos.dyalog.com/for-business/dyalog-support-service/)

-   __Consultancy__

    *Expert consulting and recruitment solutions*

    If you require more than advice from our support service, we can connect you
    with one of our consulting partners or, depending on geographical location and
    the subject matter, offer consultancy directly. If you are looking for permanent
    internal APL resources, we can advise on recruitment.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Consultancy](https://dyalogprod.gos.dyalog.com/for-business/consultancy/)

-   __Training and Workshops__

    *Customisable courses in Dyalog APL*

    No matter what your existing skill level is, we can offer training courses
    designed to help you reach the next level. Delivered both online and in-person
    globally, our courses can be customised to meet your needs, from public
    tutorials to bespoke sessions delivered at the location of your choice.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Training and Workshops](https://dyalogprod.gos.dyalog.com/for-business/training/)

-   __Escrow__

    *Verified escrow to safeguard your operations*

    If you need to convince your stakeholders that you will be able to continue to
    use Dyalog no matter what happens to Dyalog Ltd, you can subscribe to our
    fully-verified escrow offering. A copy of the source code, including the build
    and quality assurance system, will be released to escrow subscribers in the
    event that Dyalog Ltd stops supporting the product.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Escrow](https://dyalogprod.gos.dyalog.com/for-business/escrow/)

</div>

We also offer:

- **Service Level Agreements (SLAs)** — *contractually guaranteed services.* If you have specific obligations in the form of SLAs related to products based on Dyalog, we can offer contractually guaranteed levels of service to match your commitments, including guaranteed response times, problem analysis, and solutions.
- **Support for Specific Builds** — *tailored Dyalog build selection.* If your Dyalog-based product is part of a significant testing, integration, and distribution pipeline, you can appoint one or more specific builds of Dyalog as your production version and request specific patches for that build.
- **Application Mirroring** — *hosting and monitoring.* We can host or monitor a live copy of customer application(s) for immediate problem reproduction and tracking. This is particularly useful if remote access to your environment is difficult; if your application contains tests, we can also benchmark the performance of a new version of Dyalog using these.
