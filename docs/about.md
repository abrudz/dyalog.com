# About Dyalog Ltd

We are committed to advancing Dyalog and promoting its use, and have been since our
founding in 1981. Our
[dedicated team](https://dyalogprod.gos.dyalog.com/about/team-dyalog/) enjoys being
part of a passionate, supportive, and vibrant community.

Dyalog Ltd Head Office, Bramley, Hampshire, UK.
[View on Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Dyalog+Ltd/@51.331127,-1.066658,15z).

## Dyalog DNA

We are the creators of Dyalog APL, a powerful array-oriented programming language
designed for concise, expressive problem solving. Our team is dedicated to its
ongoing development and long-term maintenance, ensuring it continues to meet the
evolving needs of our users.

To support this, we provide a suite of development tools and environments that
enable both Subject Matter Experts and IT professionals to rapidly turn complex
ideas into robust, efficient software solutions.

We are proud of our traditions and
[how we got here](https://dyalogprod.gos.dyalog.com/about/our-history/), and honour
that by striving to:

- empower our users to design and build their own software tools with Dyalog.
- keep Dyalog up-to-date with advances in modern demands and technologies.
- provide no-nonsense interfaces that enable Dyalog to be fully integrated in the development and production environments.
- promote use of the APL language, and encourage anyone who has an interest to develop their knowledge.

## Judge Us by Our Actions

It is our hope that you will experience our guiding principles first hand in all
your dealings with us.

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   __Users Come First__

    *Shaped by Our Community*

    We nurture close relationships with our users to ensure we can meet their needs.
    We support our online communities, host regular in-person user meetings, and
    attend local user group meetings and events across the world. Users are at the

-   __Fair and Transparent Pricing__

    *Pricing With Integrity*

    We want Dyalog to be used to its full potential. For that our pricing has to be

-   __Teamwork__

    *Shared Ideas*

    We promote a culture where innovation, fun, pride, and acceptance is part of our
    daily life. We come from a variety of backgrounds, and our diversity enhances
    our creativity.

-   __Looking to the Future__

    *Creating Together*

    Anybody can learn and use Dyalog and APL, irrespective of their prior
    programming experience. We encourage and support newcomers to APL by offering
    online tutorials, documentation, training materials, and software downloads, all
    free of charge.
