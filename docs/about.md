# About Us

Dyalog Ltd is the creator of Dyalog APL, an array-oriented programming language for
concise, expressive problem solving. Founded in 1981, we have been advancing the
language and promoting its use for over 40 years.

## What we care about

- **Users come first** — we nurture relationships through online communities, in-person user meetings, and local user groups worldwide.
- **Fair and transparent pricing** — educational and non-commercial use is free; commercial use is licensed.
- **Teamwork and diversity** — a team of around 30 people from varied backgrounds, valuing innovation, creativity, and acceptance.
- **Future-focused** — free tutorials, documentation, training materials, and downloads welcome newcomers to APL.
