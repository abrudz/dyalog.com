# Pricing and Licences

**Fair and Transparent**

Dyalog is a licensed product, with different licences required for development and
deployment. For development work, licences for commercial use have an annual
subscription fee; they are free for non-commercial use. For deployment of your
applications, our pricing models allow you to choose the right licence to meet your
needs – if one of our standard licences isn't suitable, we are happy to work with
you to find a solution that fits with the way your software is deployed and used.

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   __Licences for Development__

    A Developer Licence allows you to use the Dyalog development environment for
    commercial and/or non-commercial purposes.

    [Developer Licences](https://dyalogprod.gos.dyalog.com/pricing-and-licences/developer-licences/)

-   __Licences for Deployment__

    A Run-Time Licence allows you to distribute applications developed using Dyalog
    to your users or customers.

    [Run-Time Licences](https://dyalogprod.gos.dyalog.com/pricing-and-licences/run-time-licences/)

-   __Separately-Licensed Products__

    Additional Licences cover Dyalog tools and products that are not included in
    standard Developer or Run-Time Licences.

    [Separately-Licensed Products](https://dyalogprod.gos.dyalog.com/pricing-and-licences/separately-licensed-products/)

</div>

We believe in a personal, tailored, approach; rather than a one-size-fits-all
online order, we start with a conversation to make sure you get exactly the right
solution.

## Need help selecting your licence?

Unsure which Dyalog licence suits your needs? Our licence guide will help you to
determine the most appropriate licence based on your intended use. Whether for
personal learning, research, or commercial development, we'll help you to make an
informed choice.
