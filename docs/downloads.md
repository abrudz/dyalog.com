## For your platform

Dyalog APL is available for Microsoft Windows, macOS, Linux, IBM AIX, Raspberry Pi,
and [Docker](https://hub.docker.com/r/dyalog/dyalog). Installation instructions for
all platforms are in the [setup readme](https://docs.dyalog.com/20.0/setup_readme.htm),
and the [Quick Start Guide](quick-start.md) walks through your first session.
