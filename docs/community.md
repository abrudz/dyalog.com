# Community and Resources

The Dyalog and APL community is a global group of developers, researchers, and
problem-solvers united by a language that treats computation as a tool of thought.
It's smaller than some, but proportionally far more active — spanning academia and
industry, and every level from curious beginner to language designer.

## Channels

- [Dyalog Forums](https://forums.dyalog.com) — discussion, troubleshooting, and knowledge-sharing
- [FAQs](https://dyalogprod.gos.dyalog.com/faqs) — answers to common questions
- [Blog](https://dyalogprod.gos.dyalog.com/blog/) — news and updates from the Dyalog team
- [News](https://dyalogprod.gos.dyalog.com/news/) — the latest announcements

## Events

- [Dyalog User Meetings](https://dyalogprod.gos.dyalog.com/user-meetings/) — the annual gathering for APL developments and applications
- [APL Seeds](https://dyalogprod.gos.dyalog.com/user-meetings/) — a free annual online meeting for beginners
- [Event Calendar](https://dyalogprod.gos.dyalog.com/community-resources/event-calendar/) — upcoming community events

## Competitions

- [APL Challenge](https://challenge.dyalog.com/) — turn ideas into code
- [APL Forge](https://forge.dyalog.com/) — build a project, win prizes

## Good apples (external)

- [Stack Overflow](https://stackoverflow.com/questions/tagged/apl+or+dyalog) — Dyalog and APL Q&A
- [r/apljk](https://www.reddit.com/r/apljk/) — the APL/J/K subreddit
